I study how digital tools can improve addiction treatment. I develop and test digital interventions, and I use large datasets and meta-analysis to find out what works, for whom, and under which conditions.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Psychology from the University of Crete and a Research Master's in Clinical and Developmental Psychopathology from Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, where I also completed my PhD on digital mental health interventions for hard-to-reach populations.

ISGF

Digital Health Interventions & Addiction

At ISGF I evaluate digital tools that help people change harmful behaviours and improve their wellbeing. I want to know what makes these interventions effective and how to keep people using them over time. The work covers substance use and behavioural addictions, and I use clinical trials, systematic reviews, and meta-analyses to test how well the treatments work.

ZKJF

Youth Forensic Mental Health

Since joining the Centre for Child and Adolescent Forensic Psychiatry and Psychotherapy in 2024, I have focused much of my research on young people in forensic mental health settings. About 84% of youth in juvenile facilities report having used drugs, and around 60% meet criteria for a substance use disorder in the year before entering custody. Yet only about 28% have ever received treatment. That gap drives much of my current work.
